#Evolvia Badges and Achievements
## 1.Purpose
Badges reward users for reaching specific achievements.
Badges encourage:
- Regular coding 
- Challenge completion
- Error fixing
- Coding Consistency
- Project progress
- Long-term learning
---
## 2.Badge Rules
A badge can only be awarded once to a user.
Once awarded, the badge remains permanently unlocked.
---
## 3.Coding Badges
|Badge|Requirement|
|---|---|
|First Code|Complete first valid coding activity|
|Code Explorer|Complete 10 coding activities|
|Code Master|Complete 50 coding activities|
---
## 4.Streak Badges
|Badge|Requirements|
|---|---|
|3 Day Streak|Reach a 3 day streak|
|7 Day Streak|Reach a 7 day streak|
|30 Day Streak|Reach a 30 day streak|
|100 Day Streak|Reach a 100 day streak|
---
## 5. Challenge Badges
|Badge|Requirement|
|---|---|
|First Challenge|Complete 1 Challenge|
|Challenge Hunter|Complete 10 Challenges|
|Challenge Master|Complete 50 Challenges|
---
## 6.Error Fixing Badges
|Badge|Requirement|
|---|---|
|First Fix|Fix 1 valid error|
|Debugger|Fix 10 errors|
|Debugging Master|Fix 50 errors|
---
## 7.Project Badges
|Badge|Requirement|
|---|---|
|First Milestone|Complete 1 project milestone|
|Project Builder|Complete 5 project milestones|
|Project Finisher|Complete 1 project|
---
## 8.Badge Duplication
The same badge must never be awarded twice to the same user.
---
## 9.Validation
Badge progress must be calculated from valid evolvia activities.
Invalid activities must not unlock badges.
